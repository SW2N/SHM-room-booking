1.Getting started

Open the booking page and bookmark it. The link stays the same every year:

https://sw2n.github.io/SHM-room-booking/

It works on a computer and on a phone, in Thai or English.

Create your account (first time only)

  1. Click Create account.
  2. Enter your company email (@sahamitproduct.com only), a password of at least 6 characters, your name and your department.
  3. That's it: you can book straight away.

Signing in

  • You stay signed in on that browser until you press Sign out.
  • After signing out, your email is filled in for you next time, and your browser can save the password.
  • Forgot your password? Click Forgot password and follow the link sent to your email.

Colours

  • Solid violet: your bookings
  • Light blue: other people's bookings
  • Striped amber: someone is filling in the booking form for this slot right now
  • Hatched: closed (weekend or public holiday)

Everything updates live: when a colleague books, you see it within seconds without refreshing.

3.Find a free room

There are two ways, depending on what you know first.

A. I know the time: which room is free?

  1. In the search bar, choose the date, start and end.
  2. Optional: tap a size (S 4–6, M 6–8, L 8–10, XL 10+ seats).
  3. Each room now shows Free or Taken. The chosen time is outlined in blue on the calendars.
  4. Click a free room, then click the outlined time to book it.

B. I know the room: when is it free?

  1. Click the room in the list on the left.
  2. Look at its calendar on the right. Switch to Work week or Month to see further ahead. In Month view, click a day to open it.
  3. Click an empty time (or drag across several half-hours) to book it.

Tip: the all-rooms view

The All rooms on this day timeline at the bottom shows every room at once. Click any empty spot to book that room at that time.

4.Book a room

  1. Click a free time (in the room calendar or the all-rooms view). The booking form opens with the room and time filled in.
  2. Check the room, date, start and end. Change them if needed.
  3. Type the Meeting title, e.g. "Sale Meeting PCB".
  4. Press Book. It's confirmed straight away, and the page moves to that room and date so you can see it.
While the form is open, the slot is held for you. Others see it as Being booked and can't take it. If someone else is filling in a form for the same slot, wait: your form checks again every 10 seconds and unlocks when the slot is free.

Rules

  • Hours: 08:00–20:00, in 30-minute steps, Monday to Friday. Weekends and public holidays are closed.
  • You can book up to 3 months ahead.
  • Two people can never book the same room at the same time. If you get "taken", someone booked it first.
  • Need a weekend, a holiday or more than 3 months ahead? Ask an admin.

Repeat bookings (e.g. a weekly meeting)

  1. In the form, open Repeat and choose Every working day, Weekly or Monthly.
  2. For weekly: choose the weekdays and how often (every 1 week, every 2 weeks…). The first date's weekday is already ticked.
  3. Set Until (the last date), then press Book.
  4. If some dates can't be booked (taken, holiday, a month without that date…), you'll see which ones and why. All the other dates are booked.

5.Check in: important

Every booking must be checked in, or the room is released.
  1. On the day of the meeting, open your booking (click it, or use My bookings).
  2. Press Check in. You can do this any time that day, up to 15 minutes after the start.
If…Then…
Nobody checks in by 15 minutes after the startThe booking becomes a no-show and the room is released for others.
You miss 2 bookings in a rowYour booking is paused for 4 hours, and your future bookings that aren't checked in are cancelled, including repeat bookings. A pop-up tells you why and when the pause ends.
You check inYour missed count goes back to zero.

Not going to use the room? Cancel it, or hand it over to a colleague, so others can use it.

6.Change, cancel or hand over

Click your booking to open it. You can only change your own bookings.

ButtonWhat it does
EditChange the room, date, time or title. Once the meeting has started, only the title and end time can change (e.g. to run longer).
Cancel bookingFor a repeat booking, choose This date only or Whole series.
Hand overGive the booking to a colleague, e.g. before leave or a business trip. Enter their company email; for a repeat booking choose this date or the whole series. They become the owner straight away and must check in. A booking can't be handed over once it has started.

7.My bookings

Press My bookings at the top to see all your upcoming bookings.

  • Bookings a colleague handed to you appear at the top. A red number on the button tells you there are new ones.
  • Today's bookings have a Check in button right there.
  • Click any booking to open it.

8.Settings: language and dates

Press Settings to choose:

  • Language: Thai or English
  • Year: Buddhist (พ.ศ.) or Western (ค.ศ.)
  • Date format: 7-Apr-98 / 7-เม.ย.-41 or 7/4/1998 / 7/4/2541

Your choice is saved to your account, so it follows you to any computer or phone.

9.On your phone

  • Same link and same account as on your computer.
  • Settings and Sign out are in the ☰ menu at the top right.
  • In the all-rooms view, swipe left and right to see other hours. Room names stay in place.
  • Tip: add the page to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) to open it like an app.

10.For admins

Admins see an Admin button (on phones it's in the ☰ menu). Admins can also open, edit, cancel, hand over and check in anyone's booking, and book weekends, holidays and more than 3 months ahead.

Holidays

  1. Pick the Year.
  2. Edit directly in the table: change a date or name, + Add row, or ✕ to remove. Changed rows are highlighted.
  3. Or use Excel: Download Excel → edit the file → Upload Excel. Columns: Date · Thai name · English name. Dates can be Excel dates or typed like 13/4/2570 or 13-Apr-27. Rows the system can't read are listed by row number.
  4. Check the table, then press Save changes. Nothing is saved before that (Discard undoes your edits).
Add next year's holidays before January so staff can't book them by mistake.

Paused accounts

Lists people whose booking is paused after 2 no-shows in a row. Press Lift ban to end a pause early.

Remove account

For someone who registered with a colleague's email by mistake. Enter the email and press Remove account; their bookings are removed too.
